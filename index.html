About the design

AxiSteer is a folding white cane with a small steerable wheel unit fitted near the base. A servo motor turns the wheel to help guide the user along a path.

The design keeps the familiar feel of a white cane. It adds a compact, enclosed housing and a 100 mm wheel.

How it works

  1. The wheel sits inside a housing that is roughly 150 mm tall.
  2. A servo motor inside the housing turns the wheel left or right.
  3. The servo attaches to the lower beam of the cane with a swivel ball joint. The joint lets the cane move freely while the wheel is steered.
  4. The cane folds down for storage, like a standard folding white cane.
